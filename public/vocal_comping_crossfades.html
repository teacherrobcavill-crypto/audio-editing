2 Why Comping Matters in Component 4

In Logic Pro's Quick Swipe Comping (and Pro Tools Playlists), selecting regions across multiple takes automatically inserts short equal-power crossfades at every edit boundary so transitions are click-free and seamless—preserving natural phrasing without destructive edits.
